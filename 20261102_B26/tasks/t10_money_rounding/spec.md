Implement `round_amount(amount: Decimal | str | int, places: int = 2) -> Decimal` and `split_amount(total: Decimal | str | int, parts: int) -> list[Decimal]` in `money/rounding.py`. `round_amount` converts the amount to a `Decimal` rounded to `places` decimal places, and the result always carries exactly that many digits after the point (so "5" becomes `Decimal("5.00")`). Ties are rounded half to even (banker's rounding). Both functions reject `float` arguments with `TypeError`, because floats cannot represent cents exactly. `split_amount` first rounds the total to 2 places with `round_amount`, then splits it into `parts` amounts with 2 decimal places that are as equal as possible and add up exactly to the rounded total, giving any leftover cents one each to the earliest parts (so "10.00" in 3 parts is 3.34, 3.33, 3.33).
