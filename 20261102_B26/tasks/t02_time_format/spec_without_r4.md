Implement two functions in `timefmt/formatting.py`. `format_timestamp(ts: float) -> str` formats a Unix timestamp as `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS UTC` (for example `2024-03-05 14:07:09 UTC`), dropping any fractional seconds. The timestamp is always rendered in UTC, whatever the local time zone of the machine running the code. `format_duration(seconds: int) -> str` writes a duration as hours, minutes and seconds such as `1h 2m 5s`, leaving out units that are zero (`3600` gives `1h`, `61` gives `1m 1s`), never rolling hours over into days, and returning `0s` for zero.
